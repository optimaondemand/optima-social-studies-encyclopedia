<!-- section: PART 1 AN OBLIGATION HE NEVER SOUGHT -->

<!-- block 10 role=content -->
Moses Austin secured his colonization grant in January 1821. Six months later, he died. Stephen F. Austin, his son, inherited the obligation to carry out a project he had not personally negotiated and had not personally wanted, left unfinished only six months after his father secured it. What Stephen does with that inheritance is where the colony that becomes Texas actually begins.
