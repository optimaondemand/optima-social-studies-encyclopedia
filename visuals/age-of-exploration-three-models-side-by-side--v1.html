📚 PART 1 · THREE MODELS

Each model below is a different answer to the same question: what do you do about the people already living on land you want? None of the three is the "normal" one — each grew out of what that empire needed and what kind of place it expected to find.

🌅 Spanish
Conquer the existing population, convert it to Christianity, and incorporate it as labor. This model needs a large, settled population already in place.
⛣ French
Send relatively few settlers. Trade, form alliances, and intermarry with Native nations rather than conquering them.
🏙️ English
Transplant whole communities across the ocean to settle permanently, hold land apart from Native peoples, and displace them from it.
Each model is an answer to a problem, and each fits a different kind of place.

Why the model matters more than the map

A model built to conquer an existing empire will not work in a place with no empire to conquer. A model built around a handful of traders will not populate a continent. Keep this in mind: the question is not only which power reaches Texas first, but which model actually fits what Texas turns out to be.
